    A product team was about to roll a vibe-coded HR platform out to the whole company with no onboarding and no UX input. I turned a team that didn't want UX into one that commissioned three rounds of research, led the redesign of the core flows, and the redesigned tool shipped company-wide.

  3. ColorPro: turning a product catalogue into a creator community

    A professional-monitor brand's site sold specs to people who buy on identity. I led the UX of a community-first rebuild (regional partner surveys, flows, wireframes, a tiered sign-up system), and after relaunch, page views rose over 400% and the community grew past 17,000 members in its first year.

    +400% Page views after relaunch measured

  4. Adapted Energy: making remote delivery credible to an expert audience

    An offshore-energy consultancy was selling something its expert buyers instinctively reject: that critical offshore scope can be delivered remotely. Working through an agency, I designed the site that had to make that credible (one navigation serving sceptical clients and the specialists being recruited) and shipped the design system and screens.
