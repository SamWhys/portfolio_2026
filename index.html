About

The short version

I'm Sam Perniskie, a senior product designer based in Taipei. For six-plus years I've owned product and web experiences end to end, currently as the global UX/UI team lead at a display-technology manufacturer, where I founded and lead the global design system.

There's a process to these things, and I like owning it end to end: making the early case for why something should exist, winning the buy-in to get it off the ground, then seeing it through discovery, design, and the handoff into engineering. That arc is where design systems and accessibility live, and where I've done my most useful work.

I use AI tools daily (prototyping, research synthesis, data analysis) and the judgment stays human. Accessibility is baked in, not bolted on: this site holds itself to WCAG 2.2 AA and says so out loud.
