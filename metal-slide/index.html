Metal Slide: a click-through story game

In story scenes, press the right arrow, D, Space, Enter, Z, X or J, or click or tap, for the next line, and the left arrow, A, Page Up or Backspace to go back. Between scenes you play: the arrow keys or A and D move, Up, W, X, K or Space jump, Z, J or Enter fire Sam's weapon, which changes with each era, from a slingshot in the cave to a laser in the future, and Down or S crouches. Page Down also works: in play, the first press runs Sam there by himself and the second skips ahead. On a touch screen, tap for the next line and swipe right to go back; in play Sam runs by himself, a tap fires, a swipe up jumps and a swipe down crouches. M mutes, F is fullscreen, 1 to 5 jump to a chapter and 0 to the title. After the title comes a world map, How I transformed the company: each chapter is a button that opens a short description with Play now and pathways to the case studies behind it, and the World map button brings you back to it. If nobody plays for 30 seconds the game plays itself; any key takes over again, and the Watch the story button turns that on or off. Escape or the Options button opens options: turn off the idle demo, reduce motion, or turn off the letter-key shortcuts. Each line of dialogue is read out below the game, and you are told when it is your turn to play. Some missions end with links to the case study behind them, after the game's buttons.
